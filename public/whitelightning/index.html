THE FULL RUNDOWN
Hang the tail out to match every curve. Green is safe but slow; ride the needle toward the edge and she pulls up to half again harder. The packed dark middle of the road holds her steady; the pale loose stuff toward the edges rattles the needle. The ridge itself rolls under you: downgrades hand you free speed, climbs bleed it off, and the dashed contour lines mapped across the hollow tell you which is coming: long green dashes for a downgrade, short red ticks for a climb. Lantern-lit barns along the ridge are drop points: cross the chalk line and the keeper opens his counter. Sell your shine for cash at whatever the keeper's paying that night, or trade a run of jars for another continue. Roll out with a full trunk either way. A full case of twelve sells for a bonus. The revenuers race the same road behind you, faster on the straights, weaker in the curves. Bumper to bumper and it's over.

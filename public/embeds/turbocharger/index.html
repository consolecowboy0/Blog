1

Exhaust spins the turbine. Hot gas leaving the engine would normally be wasted. Instead it blasts through the turbine wheel and makes it spin.

2

The shaft links the two wheels. Turbine and compressor sit on one shaft. Spin one, the other spins at the same speed, up to ~150,000 RPM.

3

The compressor packs in air. The spinning compressor sucks in fresh air and forces it into the engine under pressure, called boost.

4

More air means more power. Extra air lets the engine burn more fuel each cycle, so a small engine punches well above its size.

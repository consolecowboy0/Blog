Hey, I'm Dustin Landers (if you didn't guess). Welcome to my corner of the internet.

The deal with this page is simple: writing. Some of it is data journalism, motorsport and politics worked over with real statistical rigor. Some of it is essays and editorials. Some of it is fiction, the kind of stories I used to write as a kid and never really quit. No single beat. The throughline is the writing itself.

I have a background in data, tech, math, stats, politics, biology- and that stuff is all fun. It feeds the work. But this blog is as much about the act of writing as it is about any one subject.

## Why a blog?

Social media is fine for quick takes. Writing worth keeping needs room to breathe. I wanted a space for longer pieces, whether that's a data investigation laid out properly or an essay that wanders where it wants. So here it is, built with [Astro](https://astro.build), kept minimal on purpose.

Thanks for stopping by. More to come.

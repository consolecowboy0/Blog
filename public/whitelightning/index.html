THE FULL RUNDOWN
Hang the tail out to match every curve. Green is safe but slow; ride the needle toward the edge and she pulls up to half again harder. The packed dark middle of the road holds her steady; the pale loose stuff toward the edges rattles the needle. The ridge itself rolls under you: downgrades hand you free speed, climbs bleed it off, and the dash reads it plain: red arrows climbing, green arrows dropping, elevation in feet alongside. A painted plank warns of the curve ahead before you're in it. Lantern-lit barns along the ridge are drop points: cross the chalk line and the keeper opens his counter. Sell as many jars as you want for cash at whatever the keeper's paying that night, buy jars back at that same price to restock the trunk, or trade a run of jars for another continue. A full case, sold all at once, earns a bonus. Load heavier at the hideout and she hauls more cash but drives like it. The revenuers race the same road behind you, faster on the straights, weaker in the curves. Bumper to bumper and it's over. Run it in daylight and the law drives a little harder to keep up.

Desert Rally

A silver WRX, an open desert, and a track with no end.
A packed-dirt rally loop winding through the open desert. The car works its own throttle and brake — you only steer. Hang the tail out to match every curve. The packed dark middle of the track holds her steady; the loose pale sand toward the edges rattles the needle. Ride it clean and she pulls up harder; get twitchy and she'll break loose. The desert rolls under you: downgrades hand you free speed, climbs bleed it off. Keep four wheels on the dirt — drop two into the deep sand and you might spin it into the scrub. See how far you can run.
